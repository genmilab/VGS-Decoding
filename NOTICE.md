# Source and asset provenance

This package reorganizes the corrected VGS inference utilities from the source
VASE/VGS research workspace into a standalone implementation for LLaVA-Med and
MedGemma on VQA-RAD. The source workspace's MIT copyright and permission notice
is preserved in LICENSE; it is not an assertion about authorship of the VGS
research paper.

The website includes all twelve author-provided manuscript figure assets,
including the architecture, results and token-category plots, motivating
example, and six appendix case images. They are reproduced from
`paper/latex/images/` in the source workspace. PDF figures also have PNG
renderings for browsers. Original asset content is unchanged; accompanying
questions and model responses are transcribed from the supplied manuscript.
Third-party marks and illustrative medical imagery within the manuscript
figures retain their respective rights; the software license does not relicense
third-party content.

`docs/assets/genmilab-logo.png` is the public organization avatar linked from
[github.com/genmilab](https://github.com/genmilab), downloaded on 2026-09-21
from `https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/285129667?s=512&v=4` and included
unchanged at the project author's request. It is lab branding, not a paper
figure, and remains subject to its owner's rights.

No pretrained weights, model access credentials, complete datasets, experiment
prediction files, or evaluation scripts are distributed. The six selected
appendix images and their displayed reference/model answers are included only
as the author-requested paper illustrations described above.
Third-party model and dataset licenses and access terms remain separate from
this code license. The LLaVA-Med default is a community Hugging Face-format
conversion, identified explicitly in the README.

Code release contributors: Govinda Kolli (`govindakolli`) and Adinath Madhavrao
Dukre (`adinathdukre`). Publishing target: `genmilab/VGS-Decoding`. Contributor
credit is separate from the paper's full author list and the preserved source
license notice.
